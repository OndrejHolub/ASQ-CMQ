About this reference

A structured study companion for the ASQ CMQ/OE examination. Each chapter presents key concepts drawn from the Body of Knowledge, followed by flashcard review and a 15-question practice quiz. Navigate using the sidebar.

How to Use This Reference

Each chapter is organized into three parts:

Content
Detailed notes covering every testable topic in the BoK, written to the depth expected on the exam. Key terms are bolded; distinctions are presented in comparison tables.
Flashcards
Fifteen concept-level questions. Click a card to flip to the answer. Use the arrow buttons to advance. Review until every answer comes without hesitation.
Practice Quiz
Fifteen ASQ-style multiple choice questions with answer explanations. Complete each quiz without referring to notes before reviewing your results.
Exam Note

The ASQ CMQ/OE examination is open-book. Familiarity with structure and location of key concepts matters as much as memorization.
